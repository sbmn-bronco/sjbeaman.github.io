Method and definitions
Support
Share of all baskets containing the full itemset (left and right side together).
Confidence
Of baskets containing the left side, the share that also contain the right side.
Lift
Confidence divided by the right side's own support. Above 1× means a real affinity; below 1× means the items avoid each other.
Rule mining
All two-item pairs with support of at least 0.4% (about 40 baskets) and three-item sets with support of at least 0.3%, built from items in at least 2% of baskets. Both directions of each pair are listed.
Departments
Each product group was assigned to a department by hand for this analysis; the dataset itself has no category field.
Left out
The data has no dates, prices or customer IDs, so there is no trend, revenue or loyalty view.
