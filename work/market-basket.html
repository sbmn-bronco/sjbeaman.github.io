How to read the measures

Support

Share of all baskets that contain the whole rule. Tells you how often the combination happens at all.

Confidence

Of the baskets with the first item(s), the share that also contain the other. Reads as "buyers of A also buy B x% of the time".

Lift

Confidence divided by how common B is on its own. Above 1 means A and B are bought together more than chance; 2 means twice as likely.

Source: groceries.csv, 9,835 point-of-sale transactions across 169 product groups, each assigned to one of 11 departments.
