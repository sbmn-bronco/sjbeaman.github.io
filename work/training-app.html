Program overview

8-Week Strength + Cut. 200 → 188 lb, bigger squat and power clean, glute growth. 4 required days + 1 optional, 75 minutes or less each.

Weekly layout

  • Day 1 Hang power clean speed EMOM, back squat, glute tri-set, short WOD
  • Day 2 Heavy power clean, clean pulls, bench and upper, arms, WOD
  • Day 3 Glute and posterior chain, leg curls, arm superset, intervals
  • Day 4 Clean complex, front squat, lunge and leg-curl superset, longer WOD
  • Day 5 (optional) Clean technique, power snatch, hamstrings and arms, Zone 2

Phases

  • Weeks 1–3 Build: volume at 70–79%
  • Week 4 Deload: lighter, recover, keep the cut going
  • Weeks 5–7 Intensify: heavier triples and doubles at 80–90%
  • Week 8 Test: power clean 1RM (Day 1), benchmark WOD retest (Day 2), back squat 1RM (Day 4)

Nutrition for −12 lb in 8 weeks

  • Deficit: about 750 kcal below maintenance (1.5 lb a week)
  • Start at: about 2,300–2,400 kcal a day, then adjust after 2 weeks of weigh-ins
  • Protein: 180–200 g a day. This is the biggest lever for keeping strength and building glutes in a deficit.
  • Carbs: most of them before and after training; higher on Days 1, 2 and 4
  • Adjust: losing under 1 lb a week for 2 weeks, cut about 150 kcal. Losing over 2 lb a week or lifts stalling hard, add about 150 kcal.

RPE is effort out of 10 (8 means 2 reps left). No running and no wall balls: swap any cardio piece freely between row, bike and ski. Keep cleans and snatches in WODs at 65% or lower so fatigue doesn't groove bad technique. Abs every day, 5–6 minutes after the WOD.
